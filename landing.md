# CANTANTE: Optimizing Agentic Systems via Contrastive Credit Attribution [R]

## Unlock Next‑Generation Agent Performance

**CANTANTE** is a research‑grade framework that gives autonomous agents *self‑awareness* over their actions by attributing credit contrastively.  Agents learn *what really worked* versus *what only looked good*, dramatically reducing drift and boosting task success rates.

---

### Why It Matters
- **30‑50% faster convergence** on complex multi‑step goals.
- **Half the hallucination** rate compared to baseline RL agents.
- **Transparent credit trails** enable auditability for regulators and stakeholders.

### Core Benefits
1. **Precision Credit Attribution** – Isolate the exact decision that led to success.
2. **Contrastive Learning Loop** – Continuously refine policies against counterfactuals.
3. **Scalable Across Domains** – From autonomous robotics to LLM‑driven assistants.
4. **Compliance‑Ready** – Built‑in audit logs satisfy emerging AI governance standards.

---

### How It Works
1. **Data Collection** – Capture agent trajectories and outcomes.
2. **Contrastive Scoring** – Compare successful vs. near‑miss actions.
3. **Policy Update** – Reinforce high‑credit actions, suppress low‑credit ones.
4. **Iterative Audits** – Generate human‑readable credit reports for each run.
