## Why Traditional AI Metrics Fall Short

* **Isolated metrics** – Most teams track performance in silos (accuracy, latency, cost) without tying them to real product outcomes.
* **Static models** – Pure ML models don’t adapt to changing business contexts, leading to drift and wasted engineering effort.
* **No clear ROI** – Executives can’t see how a 0.2% accuracy gain translates into dollars.

## The Hybrid‑Model Advantage

1. **Metrics‑Driven Decision Engine** – Real‑time AI metrics (precision, recall, cost‑per‑prediction) feed directly into a business rules layer that maps to product KPIs (conversion, churn, LTV).
2. **Dynamic Weighting** – Bayesian and rule‑based components re‑balance metric importance as market conditions shift.
3. **Explainable Outcomes** – Stakeholders get clear, actionable insights: *"Improving model latency by 30 ms will increase checkout conversion by 1.4 %".*
