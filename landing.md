## 1. Subscription Memberships & Communities
- **Platforms:** Patreon, SubscribeStar, Memberful, Discord paid tiers.
- **Key Tactics:** Tiered perks, exclusive behind‑the‑scenes content, community challenges.
- **Revenue Example:** 5,000 members @ $7/mo = **$35k/mo**.

## 2. Direct Product Sales (Digital & Physical)
- **Digital:** E‑books, courses, templates, NFTs.
- **Physical:** Branded merch, limited‑edition drops.
- **Conversion Boost:** Bundle bundles, limited‑time offers, pre‑order hype.

## 3. Brand Partnerships & Sponsored Content
- **Micro‑Deals:** Affiliate links, discount codes.
- **Mid‑Tier:** Sponsored videos, podcast spots, dedicated newsletters.
- **Enterprise:** Long‑term ambassadorships, co‑created products.

## 4. Live Experiences & Events
- **Virtual:** Paid webinars, workshops, live‑stream tickets.
- **In‑Person:** Meet‑ups, pop‑up shops, speaking gigs.
- **Hybrid:** Ticketed live streams with exclusive merch.

## 5. Community‑Driven Crowdfunding
- **Platforms:** Kickstarter, Indiegogo, GoFundMe for creator projects.
- **Best Practices:** Early‑bird rewards, stretch goals, transparent updates.

## 6. AI‑Powered Services
- **Offerings:** Custom AI‑generated content, chatbots, consulting on AI tools.
- **Why Now:** AI tools are mainstream; creators can monetize expertise.
