## The 5 Types of Business Integration

| # | Integration Type | Core Benefit | Ideal For |
|---|-------------------|--------------|-----------|
| 1️⃣ | **Data Integration** | Unified, real‑time data across apps | Companies with fragmented spreadsheets & databases |
