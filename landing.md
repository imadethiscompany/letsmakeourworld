## ⚡ Why This Deal Matters

- **Scale:** A $66.8 billion transaction – the largest clean‑energy merger in U.S. history.
- **AI Integration:** NextEra plans to embed AI‑powered grid management, predictive maintenance, and demand‑response across Dominion’s 30,000 MW portfolio.
- **Renewables Boost:** The deal adds 12 GW of solar & wind assets, pushing the combined clean‑energy capacity past 30 GW.
- **Investor Signal:** Wall Street sees AI as the next competitive edge for utilities; shares of both companies rallied 8% on the news.

## 🔍 How NextEra Will Use AI

1. **Predictive Maintenance:** Machine‑learning models forecast equipment failures 30‑60 days in advance, cutting downtime.
2. **Dynamic Load Balancing:** Real‑time AI algorithms shift power between solar, wind, and storage to meet demand spikes.
3. **Carbon‑Smart Dispatch:** AI selects the lowest‑carbon generation mix for each hour, helping meet ESG targets.
