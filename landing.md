# ShadowCat

## Transfer Files Instantly with QR Codes – No Apps, No Installations

**Upload a file, generate a QR code, and scan to download – all in your browser.**

### Why ShadowCat?
- **Zero friction:** Works on any modern browser, no extensions needed.
- **Secure & private:** Files are encrypted end‑to‑end and deleted after download.
- **Lightning fast:** Transfer up to 2 GB in seconds via peer‑to‑peer WebRTC.
- **Cross‑platform:** iOS, Android, Windows, macOS – just scan.

### How It Works
1. **Drag & drop** your file onto the ShadowCat page.
2. **Generate** a QR code that encodes a secure download link.
3. **Scan** the QR with any device – the file downloads directly.

### Perfect For
- Developers sharing builds.
- Designers sending assets to mobile devices.
- Remote teams exchanging large files securely.
