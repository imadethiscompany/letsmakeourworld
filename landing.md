*The city that gave us the personal computer, the internet, and the mobile revolution is now buzzing with a new kind of excitement—Artificial Intelligence. But this wave feels unlike any before. ## 📍 The Historical Context: SF as a Tech Trailblazer

- **1970s – The Birth of Silicon Valley**: From the first microprocessor to the rise of **HP** and **Intel**, San Francisco and the Bay Area set the groundwork for modern computing.
- **1990s – The Internet Boom**: Companies like **Yahoo!**, **eBay**, and **PayPal** turned the city into a global digital marketplace.
- **2000s – Mobile & Social**: **Twitter**, **LinkedIn**, and **Uber** re‑defined how we communicate and move.

*Each wave reshaped the economy, but AI feels different because the **foundational layers**—data, compute, and talent—are now **mature, abundant, and cheap**.*

## 🤖 Why AI Feels Different Now

| Factor | Past Waves | AI Today |

These five pillars converge **only in San Francisco** where capital, talent, and a culture of rapid iteration intersect.
