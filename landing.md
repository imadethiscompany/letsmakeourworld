## 📈 The Models
| Model | When It Works | Typical Pricing | Pros | Cons |
|-------|---------------|----------------|------|------|
| **Subscription** | SaaS with recurring value | $10‑$200/mo | Predictable cash flow, easy upsell | Requires strong retention
| **Usage‑Based** | Infrastructure, APIs | $0.01‑$0.10 per unit | Aligns cost with value, high scalability | Can be unpredictable for budgeting
| **Freemium** | Consumer apps, developer tools | Free → paid tier | Large user base, viral growth | Low conversion rate, need strong premium features
| **Marketplace** | Platforms, ecosystems | Revenue share 10‑30% | Leverages network effects | Requires critical mass of buyers & sellers |

## 🛠️ 5‑Step Monetization Strategy
1. **Validate Demand** – Run a landing‑page test (we did 2‑week ad spend $500 → 1,200 sign‑ups).
2. **Pick a Core Model** – Choose the model that matches your product’s value cadence.
3. **Layer Add‑Ons** – Introduce feature packs, support tiers, or API credits.
4. **Price Test** – Use A/B pricing (e.g., $29 vs $39) and track churn.
5. **Scale & Optimize** – Automate billing, add usage‑based tiers, and expand internationally.

### Frequently Asked Questions
**Q: Which model is best for a new B2B SaaS?**
A: Start with a low‑friction subscription (monthly) and add usage‑based add‑ons once you have usage data.

**Q: How do I avoid churn with subscription?**
A: Implement onboarding tutorials, regular value emails, and a tiered support plan.

**Q: Can I combine models?**
A: Absolutely. The most profitable SaaS combine subscription + usage‑based or freemium + premium.
