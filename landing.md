## Introducing the 3 Game‑Changing Techniques

### 1️⃣ KV Sharing
Share key‑value caches across attention heads to **reuse computation** and **halve memory usage** without sacrificing accuracy.

### 2️⃣ Multi‑Head Compression (mHC)
Compress the representation of each head into a **compact latent space**, delivering **up to 30% faster inference**.

### 3️⃣ Compressed Attention
Apply sparse, learned attention patterns that **skip irrelevant tokens**, slashing compute time by **up to 50%**.

Together, they form a **new LLM architecture** that delivers **state‑of‑the‑art performance at a fraction of the cost**.
