## Why AI Coding Assistants Matter

Developers spend **30% of their time** on repetitive coding tasks—debugging, boilerplate, and searching documentation. AI coding assistants like GitHub Copilot, Tabnine, and CodeWhisperer are transforming productivity by automating these chores, reducing development cycles by up to **40%**.

## Key Growth Drivers

1. **Enterprise Adoption** – Major cloud providers bundle AI assistants into IDEs, driving bulk licensing.
2. **Productivity Pressure** – Shorter time‑to‑market is a competitive imperative for SaaS and fintech firms.
3. **Advances in LLMs** – Larger, instruction‑tuned models improve code correctness and language coverage.
4. **Regulatory Support** – AI‑assisted development is being recognized in government digital transformation programs.
