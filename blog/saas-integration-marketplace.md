## What Is a SaaS Integration Marketplace?
A SaaS Integration Marketplace is a curated hub where software vendors publish pre‑built connectors that let their product talk to other SaaS tools (e.g., Zapier, Workato, Tray.io, n8n). Buyers browse, test, and subscribe to integrations that automate their workflows.

## Key Benefits for Your Business

2. **Reduced Sales Cycle** – Prospects can self‑serve a live demo of the connector.
3. **Scalable Revenue Streams** – Charge per‑integration, per‑seat, or a revenue‑share model.
4. **Data‑Driven Insights** – Marketplace dashboards reveal which integrations drive the most usage.
