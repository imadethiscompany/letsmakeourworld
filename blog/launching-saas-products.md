## The 5‑Step Launch Framework

1. **Identify Core Integration** – Pinpoint the single workflow your target market struggles with and build a native connector (e.g., CRM ↔️ Email).
2. **Validate with a Micro‑Beta** – Invite 20‑30 power users, capture usage data, and iterate on UI/UX.
3. **Build an Automated Onboarding Funnel** – Use in‑app tutorials, pre‑filled templates, and one‑click provisioning.
4. **Deploy a Tiered Pricing Model** – Free tier for basic sync, Pro for advanced automations, Enterprise for custom APIs.
5. **Launch with a Launch‑Series Campaign** – Blog post, guest webinar, and a gated checklist download.
