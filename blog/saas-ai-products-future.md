## How to Choose the Right AI‑Powered SaaS

1. **Identify the bottleneck** – Where does your team spend the most manual effort?
2. **Check data readiness** – AI needs clean, structured data. Look for integrations that pull from your CRM, ERP, or analytics.
3. **Evaluate integration depth** – Does the solution offer native APIs, webhooks, and pre‑built connectors?
4. **Start with a pilot** – Deploy to a single team or product line, measure KPIs, then scale.

**Pro tip:** Prioritize platforms that provide a *sandbox* environment so you can test models without affecting live users.
