# NanoClaw Creator NanoCo Lands $12 Million in Seed Funding to Build Enterprise AI Agents

**Breaking News – CTech**

*Date: May 20 2026*

---

## The Funding Milestone

NanoCo, the startup behind the revolutionary **NanoClaw** AI creator platform, announced today that it has secured **$12 million in seed funding** from a syndicate of leading venture firms including **AI Ventures**, **FutureTech Capital**, and **Global Innovators**. The round is aimed at accelerating the development of **enterprise‑grade AI agents** that can be customized, deployed, and managed at scale.

---

## Why This Matters for Enterprises

- **Rapid AI Agent Creation** – NanoClaw’s low‑code builder lets product teams design agents in hours instead of months.
- **Enterprise‑Ready Security** – Built‑in data isolation, role‑based access, and compliance certifications (ISO 27001, SOC 2).
- **Scalable Infrastructure** – Powered by a distributed edge network that can handle millions of concurrent interactions.
- **Proven ROI** – Early adopters report a **30‑40 % reduction in manual workflow time** and **up to 3× faster time‑to‑value** for AI initiatives.

---

## The Vision

NanoCo’s mission is to **democratize AI agent development** for large organizations, giving them the tools to automate complex processes, improve customer experiences, and unlock new revenue streams without the need for deep ML expertise.

---

## What’s Next?

The fresh capital will be used to:
1. **Expand the engineering team** to accelerate core platform features.
2. **Launch an enterprise sandbox** for secure testing and compliance.
3. **Grow the partner ecosystem** with system integrators and consulting firms.
4. **Release a marketplace** where companies can share and monetize pre‑built agents.
