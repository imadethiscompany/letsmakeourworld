# 12 Passive Income Ideas for Developers in 2026

*Unlock the power of code to earn while you sleep.*

## 1. Create and Sell Niche SaaS Tools
Build small, subscription‑based SaaS products that solve a specific developer pain point (e.g., API rate‑limit monitors, CI/CD dashboard widgets). Once launched, the recurring revenue covers hosting and support costs while you focus on feature upgrades.

## 2. Publish Technical eBooks & Guides
Write deep‑dive eBooks on hot topics—Rust for WebAssembly, AI Prompt Engineering, or Serverless Edge Computing. Sell them on Gumroad or your own site with a one‑time payment or a subscription bundle.

## 3. Build a Paid Newsletter
Curate weekly insights on emerging dev trends, code snippets, and job opportunities. Use platforms like Substack or Revue. Offer a free tier and premium tier ($5‑$15/mo) for exclusive content.

## 4. Develop and License Open‑Source Libraries
Create a high‑quality library, then offer a commercial license for enterprises that need priority support, custom features, or a no‑license‑key version.

## 5. Monetize YouTube or Twitch Channels
Produce short tutorials, live coding sessions, or tech commentary. Enable channel memberships, Super Chat, and ad revenue. Consistency + SEO‑optimized titles can generate $200‑$2,000+ per month.

## 6. Create a Paid Community or Discord Server
Host a private Discord where members get Q&A sessions, code reviews, and early access to tools. Charge a monthly membership fee ($10‑$30).

## 7. Affiliate Marketing for Developer Tools
Write comparison articles or video reviews of cloud services, IDEs, or APIs. Include affiliate links (e.g., AWS, DigitalOcean, Linode). Earn 5‑30% commission on referrals.

## 8. Offer Pre‑Built Templates & Boilerplates
Sell ready‑made project templates—Next.js starter kits, serverless functions, or CI pipelines. Buyers can jump‑start their projects, and you earn per download.

## 9. License Your Code Snippets on Marketplaces
Upload premium snippets to sites like CodeCanyon or Gumroad. Each purchase gives you a royalty without extra effort.

## 10. Host Paid Webinars & Workshops
Run live, interactive workshops on cutting‑edge topics (e.g., Prompt Engineering with LLMs). Charge $25‑$150 per seat and record for later sales.

## 11. Build a Chrome/VSCode Extension
Create an extension that solves a workflow problem (e.g., AI‑powered code review). Offer a free version with a paid Pro upgrade.

## 12. Invest in Developer‑Focused SaaS Equity Crowdfunds
Use platforms like AngelList to invest in promising dev‑tool startups. Earn dividends or equity appreciation while you continue coding.

---

**Why These Ideas Work in 2026**
- **AI automation** reduces maintenance overhead, letting you focus on growth.
- **Developer audience** has high purchasing power and trusts peer‑generated content.
- **Subscription economy** continues to dominate, providing predictable cash flow.

**Ready to start?** Choose one idea, build a minimum viable product this week, and watch the passive income grow.
