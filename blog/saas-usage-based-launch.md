# SaaS Products Launching with Usage‑Based Pricing

**Unlock predictable growth and maximize revenue** by adopting usage‑based pricing for your SaaS solution.  Modern buyers demand flexibility – they only want to pay for what they actually use.  This page shows why a usage model is the fastest path to higher conversion, lower churn, and scalable profits.

## Why Usage‑Based Pricing Wins
- **Instant ROI proof** – Customers see cost‑benefit in real time, so they sign faster.
- **Lower entry barrier** – No large upfront contracts; the first month can start at $0‑$5.
- **Scalable revenue** – As a customer’s usage grows, your ARR grows *automatically*.
- **Competitive moat** – Competitors stuck on flat‑rate plans lose price‑sensitive prospects.

## How It Works
1. **Define a metered metric** – API calls, seats, storage, compute minutes, etc.
2. **Set tiered rates** – e.g., $0‑$10 per 1,000 calls, $0.08 per extra call.
3. **Integrate billing** – Use Stripe’s usage‑record API or Chargebee.
4. **Show a live usage dashboard** – Transparency builds trust and upsell opportunities.

## Quick Start Checklist
- Identify the core usage event that drives value.
- Model pricing tiers based on customer size.
- Build a usage‑tracking layer (webhooks, events, logs).
- Connect to Stripe’s **Usage Records** API.
- Publish a transparent usage UI on your app.

## FAQs
**Q: Will usage‑based pricing scare customers?**
A: No. Show a clear *maximum* monthly cap and a *preview* of next‑month cost.

**Q: How do I handle spikes?**
A: Set soft‑limits with alerts and offer auto‑upgrade.

**Q: Is it hard to switch from flat‑rate?**
A: Migrate existing customers with a prorated transition credit.
