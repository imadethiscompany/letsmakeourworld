## How to Choose the Right AI Tools for Your Business
1. **Identify the bottleneck** – Is it content creation, data analysis, support, or development?
2. **Start with a free trial** – Most AI platforms offer a 7‑day free tier; test ROI before committing.
3. **Measure impact** – Track time saved, cost reduction, or revenue uplift within 30 days.
4. **Scale gradually** – Integrate one tool at a time, then expand to a full AI stack.
