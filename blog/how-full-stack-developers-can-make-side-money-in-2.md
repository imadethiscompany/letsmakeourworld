## Why a Side Hustle Matters for Full‑Stack Engineers
- **Rising living costs** – the average developer salary isn’t keeping pace with housing and family expenses.
- **Skill depreciation** – new frameworks appear every month; a side project forces you to stay current.
- **Financial freedom** – an extra $1k‑$3k per month can fund travel, education, or a future startup.

## The 2‑Hour Blueprint

### 1️⃣ Identify Low‑Hanging High‑Demand Micro‑Services
| Niche | Typical Pay (per hour) | Quick‑Start Stack |
|-------|----------------------|-------------------|
| API for SaaS onboarding | $50‑$80 | Node.js + Express + PostgreSQL |
| Real‑time chat widget | $45‑$70 | Next.js + Socket.io |
| Data‑visualization dashboards | $55‑$90 | React + D3.js |

### 2️⃣ Build a Minimum Viable Product (MVP) in 90 Minutes
1. **Scaffold** – `npx create-next-app@latest my‑microservice` (5 min)
2. **Add core endpoint** – one POST/GET route that solves the niche problem (30 min)
3. **Deploy** – Vercel or Railway free tier (10 min)
4. **Test & Document** – simple README with usage example (15 min)

### 3️⃣ Monetize Immediately
- **Pay‑per‑use pricing** – charge $0.02 per API call.
- **Subscription tier** – $19/mo for 5 k calls, $49/mo for 20 k calls.
- **Marketplace listings** – publish on RapidAPI, Gumroad, or your own site.

### 4️⃣ Market in 30 Minutes
- Post a concise tweet thread with a demo GIF.
- Share in relevant Discord/Slack communities.
- Add a tiny landing page (use a pre‑made template) with a clear CTA.
