### How to Land the Best Jobs
1. **Build a Portfolio that Shows Results** – Include case studies with metrics (e.g., increased CTR by 30%).
2. **Network at Boston Meetups** – Join groups like *Boston Marketing Professionals* and *Creative Boston* on Meetup.com.
3. **Leverage Local Recruiters** – Agencies such as Robert Half, Creative Circle, and The Creative Group specialize in Boston talent.
4. **Tailor Your Resume for Each Role** – Highlight relevant tools (Figma, HubSpot, Google Ads) and industry experience.
5. **Show Your Boston Passion** – Mention local projects or community involvement to stand out.
