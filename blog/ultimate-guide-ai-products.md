## What Are AI Products?
AI products are software or hardware solutions that embed artificial‑intelligence technologies—machine learning, natural language processing, computer vision, and more—to automate tasks, uncover insights, and create new experiences. From AI‑powered chatbots that close sales 24/7 to predictive analytics platforms that forecast demand, AI products are transforming every industry.

## How to Choose the Right AI Product
1. **Identify a high‑impact problem** – Look for processes with high cost or low efficiency.
2. **Check integration compatibility** – Ensure the AI product works with your existing stack (CRM, ERP, CMS).
3. **Evaluate data requirements** – Successful AI needs quality data; pick tools that simplify data prep.
4. **Start with a pilot** – Deploy in a limited scope, measure ROI, then scale.
5. **Consider vendor support & roadmap** – Ongoing updates are crucial for AI models.
