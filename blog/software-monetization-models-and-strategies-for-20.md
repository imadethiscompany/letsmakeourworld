# Software Monetization Models and Strategies for 2020

## Introduction
The software industry has evolved dramatically over the past decade. In 2020, companies face intense competition, shifting user expectations, and a need to generate recurring revenue while staying agile. This guide breaks down the most effective **software monetization models** and **strategies** you can implement **right now** to boost growth and profitability.

---

## 1. Pure Subscription (SaaS)

- **What it is:** Customers pay a recurring fee (monthly, quarterly, or annually) for access to your software.
- **Why it works:** Predictable cash flow, higher customer lifetime value (CLTV), and easier upsell paths.
- **Key tactics:**
  - Offer a **free trial** (7‑14 days) with automatic conversion to a paid plan.
  - Use **tiered pricing** (Starter, Professional, Enterprise) to capture different budgets.
  - Implement **usage‑based add‑ons** (extra seats, premium features) to increase ARPU.

## 2. Freemium → Paid Upgrade

- **What it is:** Core features are free forever; advanced capabilities require a paid plan.
- **Why it works:** Low barrier to entry drives massive user acquisition; conversion rates improve when users hit limits.
- **Key tactics:**
  - Clearly **highlight the value gap** between free and paid tiers.
  - Use **in‑app notifications** when a user reaches a limit (e.g., “You’ve used 80% of your storage – upgrade now”).
  - Offer **annual discounts** (up to 30% off) to lock in revenue.

## 3. License + Maintenance

- **What it is:** One‑time license fee for perpetual use, plus optional annual maintenance contracts.
- **Why it works:** Appeals to enterprises that prefer CAPEX over OPEX.
- **Key tactics:**
  - Bundle **premium support** and **security updates** into the maintenance contract.
  - Provide **volume discounts** for multi‑seat purchases.
  - Use **renewal reminders** 30 days before expiration to reduce churn.

## 4. Usage‑Based (Pay‑As‑You‑Go)

- **What it is:** Customers are billed based on actual usage (API calls, compute hours, storage).
- **Why it works:** Aligns cost with value, ideal for developer‑focused platforms.
- **Key tactics:**
  - Offer a **free quota** to encourage experimentation.
  - Publish a **usage calculator** so prospects can forecast costs.
  - Implement **tiered discounts** as usage scales.

## 5. Marketplace / Platform Fees

- **What it is:** Host a marketplace where third‑party developers sell add‑ons, taking a percentage of each sale.
- **Why it works:** Generates network effects and additional revenue streams without building every feature yourself.
- **Key tactics:**
  - Provide **developer SDKs** and clear documentation.
  - Offer **co‑marketing** for top sellers.
  - Set a **reasonable commission** (15‑20%) to stay competitive.

## 6. Hybrid Models

- Combine two or more of the above (e.g., **subscription + usage‑based**). This captures baseline revenue while rewarding heavy usage.
- **Example:** A project‑management SaaS charges $20/mo per user plus $0.01 per GB of file storage.

## 7. Proven Conversion Strategies

| Strategy | Implementation Steps | Expected Impact |
|----------|----------------------|-----------------|
| **Free Trial → Auto‑Convert** | 1‑click sign‑up, email drip reminding of trial end, limited‑time discount on first payment. | ↑ Conversion by 15‑30% |
| **Social Proof Widgets** | Show live customer count, logos, and case‑study snippets on pricing page. | ↑ Trust, ↓ bounce |
| **Price Anchoring** | Display a high‑priced “Enterprise” plan first, then highlight the “Professional” plan as the best value. | ↑ Average order value |
| **Exit‑Intent Offers** | Pop‑up with a discount code when user moves cursor to close tab. | Recover 5‑10% of abandoning visitors |

## 8. Measuring Success

1. **Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)** – Track growth week over week.
2. **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)** – Keep CAC < 25% of LTV.
3. **Churn Rate** – Aim for < 5% monthly churn for SaaS.
4. **Net Revenue Retention (NRR)** – Target > 110% to indicate successful upsells.

Use a **dashboard** (e.g., ChartMogul, Baremetrics) to visualize these KPIs in real time.

## 9. Quick Checklist for Launching a New Monetization Model

- [ ] Define **target persona** and pain points.
- [ ] Choose **pricing tier structure** (free, starter, pro, enterprise).
- [ ] Build **billing integration** (Stripe, Paddle).
- [ ] Create **landing page** with clear CTA and social proof.
- [ ] Set up **email nurture flow** (welcome, trial expiry, upsell).
- [ ] Implement **analytics** (Mixpanel, GA4) to track funnel.
- [ ] Run **A/B test** on pricing and copy.
- [ ] Monitor **KPIs** weekly and iterate.
