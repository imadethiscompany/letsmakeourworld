## Why Cloudflare’s Stock Tumbled

On **[date]**, Cloudflare (NYSE: NET) reported earnings that missed analyst expectations, sending the stock down **18%** in after‑hours trading. The headline‑grabbing news wasn’t just the earnings miss – the company announced a **1,100‑person workforce reduction** as it pivots its product strategy toward AI‑driven services.

### Key Takeaways for Investors

- **Revenue Growth Slowed:** Q2 revenue rose **7% YoY**, below the consensus **9%**.
- **AI‑Centric Product Shift:** Cloudflare is reallocating resources to its **AI‑edge platform**, expecting higher margins but incurring short‑term costs.
- **Cost‑Cutting Move:** The layoff represents **≈5%** of the global headcount, aimed at improving operating efficiency.
- **Guidance Outlook:** Management now projects **2024 revenue of $1.44‑$1.48 B**, a modest increase from prior guidance.

## The AI‑Driven Strategy Explained

Cloudflare’s edge network is being re‑engineered to **run AI inference close to users**. This reduces latency for generative‑AI applications and opens new enterprise‑grade pricing tiers. While the move promises **higher‑margin SaaS revenue**, the transition requires **significant R&D spend** and a **temporary dip in profitability**.
