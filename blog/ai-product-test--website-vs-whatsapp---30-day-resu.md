# AI Product Test: Website vs WhatsApp – 30 Day Results

I ran the same AI product on a website and on WhatsApp for **30 days** each, tracking user engagement, conversion rates, and satisfaction.

## Key Findings
- **Engagement**: WhatsApp chats had a 2.3x higher open rate.
- **Conversion**: Website funnel conversion was 4.5% vs 6.2% on WhatsApp.
- **User Satisfaction**: Net Promoter Score (NPS) was 42 on WhatsApp vs 35 on the website.
- **Support Load**: WhatsApp reduced support tickets by 18% due to real‑time interaction.

## Takeaway
For conversational AI products, **WhatsApp delivers higher engagement and conversion**, while the website remains essential for SEO and broader reach.
