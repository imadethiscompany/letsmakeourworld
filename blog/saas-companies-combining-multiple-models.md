# SaaS Companies Combining Multiple Models

## Why Mix Pricing Models?
SaaS businesses are moving beyond a single subscription or usage‑based plan. By **combining multiple revenue models**—like tiered subscriptions, per‑seat pricing, usage‑based add‑ons, and outcome‑based fees—companies can:

- Capture more value from high‑growth customers
- Reduce churn by offering a path to upscale
- Align pricing with the actual ROI customers see

### The 4‑Model Playbook
| Model | When It Works | Key Benefits |
|-------|----------------|--------------|
| **Tiered Subscription** | Predictable core usage | Simple budgeting, easy upsell ladder |
| **Per‑Seat / User‑Based** | Teams that grow horizontally | Directly ties revenue to headcount |
| **Usage‑Based Add‑Ons** | Variable workloads (e.g., API calls, storage) | Pay‑as‑you‑go, lower entry barrier |
| **Outcome‑Based / Value‑Based** | High‑impact results (e.g., revenue lift) | Aligns price with delivered value, premium pricing |

## Real‑World Examples
- **CRM platforms** combine per‑seat fees with usage‑based email sends.
- **Analytics tools** add a tiered core plan plus a per‑event processing charge.
- **AI SaaS** often layers a subscription for the model access and a per‑token usage fee.
