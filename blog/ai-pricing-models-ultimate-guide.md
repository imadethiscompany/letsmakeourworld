## Types of AI Pricing Models

| Model | How It Works | Ideal For | Key Benefits |
|-------|--------------|-----------|--------------|
| **Value‑Based Pricing** | AI predicts the monetary value a customer derives and sets price accordingly. | Enterprise SaaS, high‑margin AI services. | Captures maximum willingness‑to‑pay. |
| **Usage‑Based (Pay‑As‑You‑Go)** | Price = Unit cost × Predicted usage (e.g., API calls). | API platforms, cloud AI services. | Low entry barrier, scales with growth. |
| **Tiered Subscription** | Fixed tiers (Starter, Pro, Enterprise) powered by AI‑driven feature bundling. | B2B SaaS, platforms with distinct user groups. | Predictable revenue, easy upsell path. |
| **Dynamic Pricing** | Real‑time price adjustments based on demand, competition, and user behavior. | Marketplaces, on‑demand AI compute. | Optimizes price per transaction, boosts margins. |
| **Hybrid Model** | Combines two or more models (e.g., base subscription + usage overage). | Complex products, multi‑segment markets. | Flexibility, balances predictability & fairness. |

## Choosing the Right Model for Your Business

1. **Analyze Customer Value Signals** – Use AI to score leads by potential ROI.
2. **Map Cost Structure** – Understand fixed vs. variable costs.
3. **Test with A/B Experiments** – Deploy multiple models to a subset of traffic.
4. **Iterate Continuously** – Feed pricing outcomes back into the AI engine.

> *Pro tip:* Start with a **tiered subscription** to establish baseline revenue, then layer **usage‑based add‑ons** for power users.
