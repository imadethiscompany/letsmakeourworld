# White House Considers Vetting AI Models Before They Are Released

**The New York Times** reported that the U.S. administration is weighing a groundbreaking policy: mandatory safety reviews for AI systems before they reach the public. This could reshape the entire AI ecosystem, from startups to tech giants.

---

## Why This Matters
- **National Security:** Prevent harmful AI from being weaponized.
- **Consumer Protection:** Guard users against bias, misinformation, and privacy breaches.
- **Market Stability:** Create a level playing field where responsible innovation thrives.

## What the Proposal Looks Like
1. **Pre‑Release Audits** – Independent labs evaluate model safety, fairness, and robustness.
2. **Transparency Reports** – Companies must disclose training data sources and risk assessments.
3. **Enforcement Mechanism** – The Federal Trade Commission (FTC) can issue fines or block deployments that fail the vetting.

## Who’s Behind It?
- **White House Office of Science and Technology Policy (OSTP)**
- **National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST)**
- **Industry leaders** such as OpenAI, Google, and Microsoft have already begun voluntary safety testing.

## What It Means for You
- **Businesses** will need to budget for compliance audits.
- **Developers** can leverage the new standards as a competitive advantage.
- **Investors** should watch for startups that embed safety by design.
