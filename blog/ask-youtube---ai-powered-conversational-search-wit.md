# Ask YouTube – AI‑Powered Conversational Search

**Ask YouTube** brings generative AI to video search. Using Gemini Omni, users can ask natural‑language questions and get concise answers drawn from YouTube content, with relevant timestamps and source links.

- **Conversational UI** – Type or speak your query, get follow‑up answers.
- **Gemini Omni** – Leverages Google’s multimodal model for accurate video understanding.
- **Instant playback** – Jump directly to the moment that answers your question.
