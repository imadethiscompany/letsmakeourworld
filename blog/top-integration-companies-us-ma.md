## How to Choose the Right Partner
1. **Define Scope Early** – API‑only vs. full‑stack integration.
2. **Check Industry Experience** – Healthcare, finance, manufacturing have unique compliance needs.
3. **Ask for Proof of ROI** – Case studies that show cost savings or revenue uplift.
4. **Evaluate Delivery Model** – Fixed‑price, time‑and‑material, or managed services.
5. **Consider Cultural Fit** – Agile vs. waterfall, on‑shore vs. off‑shore.
