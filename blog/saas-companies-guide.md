### 1️⃣ Product‑Led Growth (PLG)
Your product is the best salesperson. Deploy a free‑tier or trial, capture user behavior, and automate onboarding. **Result:** 30‑40% higher conversion vs. sales‑led.

### 2️⃣ Pricing That Grows With You
Use value‑based tiers, add‑on modules, and usage‑based billing. **Result:** 2‑3× increase in average revenue per user (ARPU).

### 3️⃣ Churn is the Silent Killer
Implement NPS‑driven feedback loops, proactive support, and renewal incentives. **Result:** Reduce churn from 8% to <3%.
