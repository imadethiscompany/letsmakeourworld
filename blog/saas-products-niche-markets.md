## The Proven 3‑Step Framework to Build a Niche SaaS

1. **Identify a Pain‑Point with a Passionate Community**
   - Search for Reddit threads, niche forums, or LinkedIn groups where members repeatedly complain about a manual process.
2. **Validate with a Minimum Viable Product (MVP)**
   - Build a simple web app that solves the core problem. Offer **free beta access** to 5‑10 power users in exchange for feedback.
3. **Scale via Targeted Content & Partnerships**
   - Publish SEO‑rich blog posts (like this one), guest‑write in niche publications, and integrate with existing tools the community already uses.
