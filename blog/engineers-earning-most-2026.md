## Why Engineer Salaries Are Soaring

- **AI & Automation Boom:** Engineers who integrate AI into products command premium rates.
- **Cloud & Edge Expansion:** Demand for cloud-native architects and edge computing specialists continues to outpace supply.
- **Sustainable Tech:** Green energy and carbon‑capture projects need high‑paid mechanical and chemical engineers.

## How to Jump Into These High‑Pay Roles

1. **Earn the Right Certifications** – Cloud certifications (AWS Solutions Architect – Professional) and AI/ML credentials (Google Cloud Professional Machine Learning Engineer) are fast‑track.
2. **Build a Portfolio of Real Projects** – Contribute to open‑source AI libraries, deploy a production‑grade Kubernetes cluster, or publish a research paper in quantum computing.
3. **Network in Niche Communities** – Join Discord/Slack groups for quantum computing, attend AWS re:Invent, or participate in hackathons focused on sustainability.
4. **Negotiate Strategically** – Use salary data from Glassdoor, Levels.fyi, and the table above to anchor your ask.
