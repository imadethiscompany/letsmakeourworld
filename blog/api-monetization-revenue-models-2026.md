### 1. Pay‑Per‑Use (Consumption‑Based)
- **Why it works:** Developers love paying only for what they consume. It removes upfront cost barriers and aligns price with value.
- **Key metrics:** Track `requests`, `data transferred`, and `compute seconds`.
- **Implementation tip:** Tiered pricing (e.g., 0‑100k calls free, 100k‑1M $0.001 per call, >1M $0.0008).

### 2. Subscription Plans (Flat‑Rate)
- **Why it works:** Predictable revenue for you, predictable cost for customers.
- **Best for:** High‑frequency, mission‑critical APIs (e.g., auth, payments).
- **Example:** $49/mo for up to 500k calls, $199/mo for 5M calls, custom enterprise tier.

### 3. Freemium + Premium Add‑Ons
- **Why it works:** Lower barrier to entry, upsell via advanced features.
- **Add‑on ideas:**
  - **Rate‑limit boost** – extra bursts for high‑traffic spikes.
  - **Data enrichment** – enriched responses, higher accuracy.
  - **SLA guarantees** – 99.9% uptime SLA for enterprise.

### 4. Revenue Share / Marketplace Model
- **Why it works:** Partner with SaaS platforms that embed your API and share revenue.
- **Structure:** 70/30 split, automated payouts via Stripe Connect.
- **Success story:** XYZ API grew from $5k/mo to $120k/mo in 6 months after joining a low‑code marketplace.

### 5. Transaction Fees (Value‑Based)
- **Why it works:** Align price with business outcome.
- **Ideal for:** Payments, fraud detection, conversion‑optimisation APIs.
- **Example:** 0.5% of transaction value per API call.
