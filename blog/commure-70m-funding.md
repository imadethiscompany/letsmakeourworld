## Transforming Healthcare with Agentic AI

Commure, a leader in AI-powered healthcare data integration, announced a **$70 million Series C round** led by General Catalyst, with participation from existing investors. This funding will accelerate the rollout of **agentic AI systems** that autonomously coordinate patient data, streamline clinical workflows, and unlock real‑time insights for providers.

### Why It Matters
- **Improved Patient Outcomes:** AI agents proactively surface critical health information, reducing errors and delays.
- **Operational Efficiency:** Hospitals can cut manual data entry time by up to **60%**, freeing staff for patient care.
- **Scalable Platform:** Built on secure, HIPAA‑compliant infrastructure, ready for nationwide adoption.
