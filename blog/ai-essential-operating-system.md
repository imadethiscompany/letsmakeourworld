## How to Make AI Your Operating System

1. **Assess Your Data Landscape** – Identify data sources and clean them for AI consumption.
2. **Choose the Right AI Stack** – Cloud‑based APIs (OpenAI, Anthropic), low‑code platforms, or custom models.
3. **Integrate Seamlessly** – Embed AI into existing tools via APIs, webhooks, or plugins.
4. **Monitor & Optimize** – Use dashboards to track performance, cost, and ROI.
5. **Scale Gradually** – Start with a pilot (e.g., AI‑driven support) and expand to core functions.
