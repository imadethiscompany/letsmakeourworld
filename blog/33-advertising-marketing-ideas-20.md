# 33 Advertising and Marketing Business Ideas For 20-Year-Old Entrepreneurs

*Ready to launch a profitable side hustle or full‑time agency?* This guide walks you through **33 actionable business ideas** you can start **today** with little to no cash, a laptop, and a passion for advertising and marketing.

---

## Why Advertising & Marketing?
- **High demand**: Every business needs customers.
- **Scalable**: Services can be packaged, automated, or turned into digital products.
- **Low overhead**: Mostly knowledge‑based, no inventory.
- **Creative freedom**: Blend data, psychology, and design.

## How to Choose the Right Idea
1. **Assess your skills** – Which of the above align with what you already know?
2. **Validate demand** – Use Google Trends or a quick survey in a Facebook group.
3. **Start small** – Offer a free pilot to 1‑2 clients, collect testimonials.
4. **Scale** – Package services into retainer contracts or digital products.

---

## Quick‑Start Checklist
- [ ] Pick one idea from the list.
- [ ] Define a 30‑day launch plan (target market, pricing, outreach).
- [ ] Set up a simple website or landing page (use Carrd or Webflow).
- [ ] Create a portfolio (even mock projects).
- [ ] Reach out to 10 prospects via email or LinkedIn.
- [ ] Close the first client within 2 weeks.
