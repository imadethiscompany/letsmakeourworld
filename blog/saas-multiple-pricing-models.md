## Why One‑Model SaaS Is No Longer Enough

Most SaaS founders start with a single pricing model—usually a flat subscription. While simple, it leaves money on the table:
- **Missed high‑value users** who would pay more for usage‑based features.
- **Underserved low‑usage customers** who can’t afford a full‑price plan.
- **Limited upside** when enterprise customers demand outcome‑based contracts.

Combining models lets you capture every segment of the market, increase average revenue per user (ARPU), and improve churn.

---

## The 4‑Model Playbook

| Model | When It Works | Key Benefits |
|-------|----------------|--------------|
| **Flat Subscription** | Predictable, low‑touch SMBs | Simple billing, easy onboarding |
| **Usage‑Based (Pay‑As‑You‑Go)** | Data‑intensive or variable‑usage customers | Aligns price with value, upsell potential |
| **Freemium / Free Tier** | Top‑of‑funnel acquisition | Low friction, viral growth |
| **Outcome‑Based / Value‑Based** | Enterprise with KPI contracts | Guarantees ROI, higher contract size |

### How to Blend Them
1. **Start with a Core Subscription** – the baseline for all users.
2. **Add a Usage Meter** – charge extra for premium API calls, storage, or seats.
3. **Offer a Free Tier** – limited features to drive sign‑ups and funnel to paid.
4. **Introduce Outcome Pricing** – for high‑value accounts, tie a portion of revenue to agreed outcomes (e.g., % of sales uplift).

## Quick Implementation Checklist
- **Segment your customers** by usage patterns and willingness to pay.
- **Define metrics** for usage‑based billing (API calls, GB stored, seats).
- **Create a tiered pricing page** that clearly shows the free tier, core subscription, and add‑on options.
- **Pilot outcome‑based pricing** with 1‑2 strategic accounts before scaling.
- **Instrument analytics** to track churn, LTV, and ARPU per model.

## Frequently Asked Questions
**Q: Won’t multiple models confuse customers?**
A: A clean UI and clear messaging keep the experience simple. Show the core plan first, then optional add‑ons.

**Q: How do I handle billing complexity?**
A: Most modern billing platforms (Stripe, Chargebee) natively support tiered, usage‑based, and custom contracts.

**Q: Is outcome‑based pricing risky?**
A: Start with a small percentage of the total contract and use strong KPIs to protect both sides.
