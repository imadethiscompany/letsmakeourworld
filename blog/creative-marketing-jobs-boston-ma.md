## How to Land Your Dream Creative/Marketing Role in Boston

### 1. Polish Your Portfolio (or Campaign Reel)
- **Show results** – Include metrics: CTR, ROI, engagement lifts.
- **Tailor for industry** – Highlight e‑commerce work for Wayfair, SaaS for HubSpot, etc.
- **Use a clean, mobile‑friendly site** – Recruiters often browse on phones.

### 2. Network Strategically
- **Boston Marketing Meetups** – Attend monthly events like *Boston Marketing Mixer*.
- **Alumni groups** – MIT, Northeastern, Boston University alumni networks.
- **LinkedIn** – Connect with hiring managers, comment on their posts.

### 3. Master the Local Keywords
- Optimize your LinkedIn headline with phrases like *“Boston Digital Marketing Specialist”* or *“Creative Designer – Boston, MA”.*
- Use location tags on your portfolio site (e.g., `Boston, MA` in meta tags).

### 4. Leverage Recruiters Who Specialize in Creative Talent
- **Creative Circle Boston**
- **The Creative Group**
- **Creative Talent Solutions**

### 5. Prepare for the Interview – Data‑Driven Storytelling
- Bring a **case study** that shows how you turned insights into a campaign.
- Be ready to discuss **Boston‑specific trends** (e.g., local sports sponsorships, higher education market).
