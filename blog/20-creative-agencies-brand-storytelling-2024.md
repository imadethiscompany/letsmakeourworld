## How to Choose the Right Agency
1. **Define Your Narrative Goal** – Are you aiming for brand awareness, product launch, or cultural relevance?
2. **Assess Their Expertise** – Look for case studies that match your industry and desired medium.
3. **Measure Impact** – Prioritize agencies that tie storytelling to concrete KPIs (engagement, conversion, brand lift).
4. **Cultural Fit** – The agency’s voice should echo your brand’s personality.
