## 1. Choose the Right Pricing Model

| Model | When It Works | Key Benefits |
|-------|----------------|--------------|
| **Usage‑Based** (pay‑per‑API call) | Variable workloads, developer‑focused products | Aligns cost with value, low entry barrier |
| **Tiered Plans** (Starter / Pro / Enterprise) | Broad market, clear feature sets | Predictable revenue, easy upsell |
| **Seat‑Based** (per user) | Collaboration tools, B2B teams | Simple to understand, scales with org size |
| **Value‑Based** (outcome pricing) | High‑impact AI (e.g., fraud detection) | Captures maximum willingness‑to‑pay |
| **Hybrid** (base + usage) | Complex SaaS with both fixed & variable components | Balances predictability & flexibility |

**Action:** Run a quick survey of your target customers to see which model matches their purchasing behavior.

---

## 2. Build a Tiered Feature Map

| Tier | Core AI Features | Add‑On Capabilities |
|------|----------------|-------------------|
| **Free** | Basic model access, limited calls | – |
| **Growth** | Unlimited calls, custom model fine‑tuning, analytics dashboard | Priority support |
| **Scale** | Dedicated instance, SLAs, advanced security, integration library | Dedicated CSM, on‑prem deployment |

Show a **comparative table** on your pricing page – visual clarity drives conversions.

---

## 3. Implement Smart Billing Automation

- Use **Stripe Billing** for subscription management, proration, and metered usage.
- Set up **webhooks** to adjust usage counters in real‑time.
- Offer **annual discounts (10‑15%)** to improve cash flow and reduce churn.

Automation eliminates manual invoicing errors and frees your team to focus on product.

---

## 4. Leverage Free‑to‑Paid Funnels

1. **Free trial** – 14‑day unlimited access to Growth tier.
2. **Feature unlock** – After trial, lock premium models behind a paywall.
3. **In‑product nudges** – Show ROI calculator when usage spikes.

A well‑designed funnel can **convert 20‑30%** of trial users to paid.

---

## 5. Optimize for Retention & Expansion

- **Usage alerts**: Notify customers when they’re close to limits; suggest upgrade.
- **Health scores**: Track model accuracy, latency, and usage trends.
- **Customer success**: Quarterly business reviews for Enterprise accounts.

Retention‑first mindset boosts **LTV** and reduces acquisition costs.

---

## 6. Test, Iterate, and Scale

- Run **A/B tests** on pricing, trial length, and feature bundles.
- Use **cohort analysis** to measure churn drivers.
- Scale infrastructure with **Kubernetes** or **serverless** to handle demand spikes without over‑provisioning.

Continuous experimentation keeps your pricing competitive and profitable.
