## How to Build a Stripe‑Powered SaaS Application (4‑Step Blueprint)

1. **Create a Stripe Account & Enable Billing** – Activate the *Billing* product and set up your product catalog.
2. **Integrate with a Serverless Backend** – Deploy webhook listeners on Vercel that update your user database on `invoice.paid`, `customer.subscription.created`, etc.
3. **Add a Checkout/Portal UI** – Use Stripe Checkout for one‑click payments or embed the **Stripe Customer Portal** for self‑service subscription management.
4. **Automate Revenue Ops** – Connect Stripe to HubSpot, Xero, or your own analytics stack using Zapier, Make, or custom webhooks.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I need a developer to integrate Stripe?**
A: No. Stripe provides pre‑built UI components and extensive SDKs for JavaScript, Python, Ruby, and more. Non‑technical founders can launch with no‑code tools like *Memberstack* or *Webflow* + Stripe.

**Q: How does Stripe handle failed payments?**
A: Stripe automatically retries, sends email dunning, and can trigger a webhook for custom recovery flows.

**Q: What about taxes?**
A: Enable *Stripe Tax* to automatically calculate VAT, GST, and sales tax for global customers.
