## How to Choose the Perfect Partner
1. **Map Your Data Flow** – Identify source, destination, and transformation needs.
2. **Assess Compatibility** – Does the vendor support your existing SaaS tools?
3. **Budget vs. Scale** – Choose a solution that grows with you.
4. **Support & Governance** – Look for SLAs, security certifications, and dedicated CSMs.

> **Pro Tip:** Start with a pilot integration (e.g., CRM ↔️ Marketing Automation) before a full‑scale rollout.
