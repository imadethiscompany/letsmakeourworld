## Why SaaS Platforms Are Adding Financial Services

1. **Increase Lifetime Value** – Customers who can **receive payments, hold balances, and borrow** without leaving the app spend up to **3‑5×** more than pure‑play SaaS users.
2. **Reduce Churn** – An embedded treasury creates **switching costs**; the product becomes the financial hub.
3. **New Revenue Streams** – Earn **interest margin**, **origination fees**, and **transaction fees** on every dollar that flows through your platform.
4. **Data‑Driven Credit** – Your SaaS usage data gives a **rich risk signal**, enabling better underwriting than traditional banks.

## Step‑by‑Step Playbook to Launch

1. **Validate the Market** – Survey existing customers: *Would you use an integrated credit line?* Aim for >30% interest.
2. **Choose a Banking‑as‑a‑Service Partner** – Evaluate based on API coverage, regulatory footprint, and pricing.
3. **Build the Account Layer** – Implement account creation flows, balance UI, and transaction history.
4. **Add Payments** – Enable instant payouts and card issuance; test with sandbox environments.
5. **Design Lending Products** – Start with a **soft‑limit credit line** tied to usage metrics (e.g., monthly active users, recurring revenue).
6. **Implement KYC/AML** – Embed identity verification in the signup flow; store verification tokens securely.
7. **Risk Model** – Combine SaaS usage data (login frequency, feature adoption) with traditional credit signals.
8. **Pilot with Power Users** – Offer beta credit to 5‑10 customers, gather feedback, iterate.
9. **Roll Out & Scale** – Launch publicly, monitor default rates, and optimize pricing.

## Common Objections & How to Overcome Them

- **“We’re not a bank.”** – You’re not; you’re a *platform* leveraging regulated partners via APIs.
- **“Regulatory risk is too high.”** – Partner handles licensing; you only need to follow KYC/AML guidelines.
- **“It’ll distract from our core product.”** – Start with a *minimum viable finance* (simple accounts + credit line) and iterate.
