# How a Guelph Woman Lost $14,000 in a Crypto Scam Pretending to Talk to Mr. Beast

![Guelph Woman Crypto Scam](/images/crypto-scam.jpg)

**Date:** May 2024 | **Source:** CBC News

---

## The Shocking Story
A 31‑year‑old woman from Guelph, Ontario, thought she was chatting with popular YouTube star **Mr. Beast** (Jimmy Donaldson) about a lucrative crypto investment. The scammer, posing as the celebrity’s manager, convinced her to send **$14,000** in cryptocurrency to a fake wallet.

> *“He said it was a private early‑bird token that would triple in value. I thought I was getting a once‑in‑a‑lifetime opportunity.”* – Victim, speaking to CBC.

Police confirmed the funds were transferred to an untraceable wallet and the scammer vanished.

---

## Why This Happens (And How to Spot It)
| Red Flag | What It Looks Like |
|---|---|
| **Impersonating a celebrity** | Direct messages claiming to be a star’s manager or agent. |
| **Urgent “limited‑time” offer** | “Act now or you’ll miss out!” with high‑pressure language. |
| **Requests for crypto payments** | Only accept crypto, often to “anonymous wallets.” |
| **Too‑good‑to‑be‑true returns** | Promises of 300%+ profit within weeks. |

### Quick Checklist
1. **Never send money** to anyone you’ve only met online.
2. **Verify the account** – official channels never request crypto.
3. **Search the phone number/email** – many scams are reported online.
4. **Ask a friend** – a second opinion can catch red flags.
