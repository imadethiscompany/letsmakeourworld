## The 5‑Step Blueprint to Build a Niche SaaS

1. **Identify a High‑Pain Segment** – Use Google Trends, Reddit, and industry forums to find a problem that no tool solves.
2. **Validate with a Mini‑MVP** – Build a single‑feature prototype, get 5‑10 paying beta users within 30 days.
3. **Create Laser‑Focused Messaging** – Write copy that mirrors the exact language your prospects use (e.g., “automate client intake for boutique law firms”).
4. **Price for Value, Not Cost** – Position as a *productivity multiplier* and charge 3‑5× the cost of a spreadsheet.
5. **Scale with Community** – Build a private Slack/Discord, publish weekly case studies, and let users become brand advocates.
