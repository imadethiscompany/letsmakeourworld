## A Proven 5‑Phase Integration Framework
1. **Pre‑Close Preparation** – Align leadership, set integration governance, and create a “Deal‑to‑Value” roadmap.
2. **Day‑Zero Execution** – Activate the integration office, communicate the vision, and secure quick‑win wins.
3. **90‑Day Stabilisation** – Consolidate IT, harmonise finance, and lock‑in talent.
4. **180‑Day Realisation** – Track synergies, optimise processes, and begin revenue‑cross‑selling.
5. **Beyond 12 Months – Scale & Optimize** – Institutionalise best practices, pursue further growth levers.

Each phase includes **ownership matrix**, **critical milestones**, and **risk‑mitigation tactics**.
