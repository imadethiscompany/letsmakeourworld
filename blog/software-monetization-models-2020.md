## Top 6 Monetization Models
| Model | Ideal For | Key Benefits | Typical Pricing |
|-------|------------|--------------|-----------------|
| **License (Perpetual)** | Enterprise, on‑prem | Large upfront cash, low churn risk | $5k‑$500k one‑time |
| **Subscription (SaaS)** | B2B & B2C SaaS | Recurring revenue, predictable cash flow | $10‑$500/mo per seat |
| **Freemium → Paid** | Consumer apps, developer tools | Large user base, easy upsell | Free tier, then $5‑$30/mo |
| **Usage‑Based (Pay‑As‑You‑Go)** | APIs, cloud services | Aligns cost with value, high scalability | $0.01‑$0.10 per transaction |
| **Marketplace / Revenue Share** | Platforms, app stores | No upfront cost, shared risk | 15‑30% of transaction |
| **Hybrid (License + Subscription)** | Complex products | Up‑front cash + ongoing upgrades | $2k upfront + $50/mo support |

## Choosing the Right Model for Your Product
1. **Understand your customer’s buying behavior** – Do they prefer CAPEX (upfront) or OPEX (ongoing)?
2. **Measure the value delivered** – High‑value, mission‑critical tools can command higher recurring fees.
3. **Assess sales cycle length** – Long sales cycles favor subscription or hybrid models.
4. **Test price elasticity** – Run A/B experiments on pricing tiers and packaging.

---

## 5 Proven Strategies to Maximize Revenue
1. **Tiered Plans** – Offer **Starter**, **Professional**, and **Enterprise** tiers to capture a wider market.
2. **Add‑On Modules** – Sell premium features (e.g., analytics, integrations) as add‑ons.
3. **Annual Discounts** – Incentivize commitment with **15‑20%** off annual contracts.
4. **Usage Caps & Overages** – Provide a generous free quota, then charge over‑usage.
5. **Customer‑Success Upsell** – Dedicated success managers can increase ARR by **30‑40%**.
