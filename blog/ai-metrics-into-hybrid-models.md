## Why Hybrid Models?
Traditional machine‑learning models excel at pattern recognition, while rule‑based systems provide deterministic control. Hybrid models combine the best of both worlds, delivering:

- **Higher predictive accuracy** – AI metrics fine‑tune the statistical component.
- **Robustness & interpretability** – rule‑based layers keep decisions explainable.
- **Scalable deployment** – leverage cloud AI services alongside on‑premise logic.

## Core AI Metrics to Track
| Metric | What It Measures | Why It Matters |
|--------|-----------------|----------------|
| **Precision / Recall** | Correctness of predictions | Balances false positives/negatives for business impact |
| **F1‑Score** | Harmonic mean of precision & recall | Single KPI for model quality |
| **AUC‑ROC** | Discrimination ability across thresholds | Crucial for risk‑sensitive domains |
| **Latency** | Time per inference | Ensures hybrid pipeline meets real‑time SLAs |
| **Resource Utilization** | CPU/GPU usage | Optimizes cost‑performance ratio |

## Building a Hybrid Model with AI Metrics
1. **Define Business Objective** – e.g., increase churn prediction accuracy by 15%.
2. **Select Base Models** – Choose a statistical model (logistic regression) and a rule engine.
3. **Instrument AI Metrics** – Integrate monitoring hooks (Prometheus, CloudWatch) to capture the metrics above.
4. **Iterate & Optimize** – Use metric feedback loops to adjust model weights and rule thresholds.
5. **Deploy & Scale** – Containerize the hybrid pipeline; auto‑scale based on latency metrics.
