# Comment Guidelines

We welcome thoughtful, respectful discussion. Please keep comments:
- On‑topic and relevant to the article.
- Free of profanity, harassment, or spam.
- Constructive and supportive.

**How to earn the *Community Contributor* badge:**
1. Post at least 3 meaningful comments.
2. Engage with other readers.
3. Follow the guidelines.

Your contributions help shape DeepSeek Reasonix. Thank you!
